First time opening it?

Mac: open the download, drag DragShift onto the Applications folder beside it, then open it from Applications. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Windows: if Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows says this about new apps until enough people have opened them.
