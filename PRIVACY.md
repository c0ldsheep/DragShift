# Privacy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

DragShift is made by c0ldsheep. In short: **DragShift collects nothing.** The app and the website have no accounts,
no analytics, no ads, no cookies and no trackers.

## The app (Mac and Windows)

- Your files are changed on your own computer. DragShift has no network code: it never uploads, sends or "phones
  home" with your files, their names or anything about you.
- Some features use your computer's own built-in tools, for example macOS's or Windows' text recognition, and
  translation on the Mac. Those run on the device; DragShift sends nothing anywhere.
- **What it stores on your computer:**
  - your settings and saved print presets
  - a small log for troubleshooting (on a Mac, `~/Library/Logs/DragShift.log`; on Windows,
    `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DragShift\DragShift.log`), with action names, file counts and error messages
  - a practice picture
  - a count of how many files DragShift has made for you (just a number, shown in the About tab)

  These never leave your computer. To remove them, delete the app and those files.
- Passwords you type to lock or unlock a PDF are used once, in memory, and are never saved or logged.

## Tips ("Buy me a coffee")

The About tab has a coffee cup for anyone who wants to tip. It shows a crypto address (on a Mac, as a QR code). The app draws these
itself and never goes online for them. DragShift can't see whether you sent anything, and nothing in the app is
locked or unlocked by a tip. If you do send one, it is a public blockchain payment like any other: the network, not
DragShift, records it.

## The website

- The site is a single page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no trackers or analytics, and makes no
  requests after it loads.
- To open the page offline later, your browser may keep a copy of the page's own files on your device. You can
  clear it in your browser's settings.
- GitHub hosts the page and the downloads, and its servers handle your request the way any web host does (for
  example, your IP address in their logs). See the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- DragShift sees only the total number of downloads per file, which GitHub reports for every release. It never
  sees who downloaded.

## Children

DragShift doesn't collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

## Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the change ships. Questions: open an issue on the
[DragShift repository](https://github.com/c0ldsheep/DragShift/issues).
